**Secure your Legacy and Protect your loved ones from the frustration of not knowing where your information resides.**
Using Legacy Pack, you can organize your legal directives, estate documents, financial information, access to your digital assets and final wishes in one central, private and secure space.
Upload copies of your executed Will, Advance Health Directive, and Powers of Attorney into Legacy Pack. All files are encrypted on your local computer with nothing stored in the cloud.

Legacy Pack will prompt you to ensure your will and other legal documents are current and up to date.
It enables you to store the details of your assets and liabilities, including your digital assets, collections, vehicles etc.
You can store the details of your accounts with Banks, Superannuation, Utilities and all other services so that outgoings can be monitored and managed.
Legacy Pack can also document your family tree, record critical contacts (doctors, accountants, lawyers etc), your funeral wishes, memorial plans, messages, and any special requests.  You can also record how you want your digital assets (online Photos, documents etc) to be dealt with.

Let Legacy Pack give you the peace of mind in knowing you have done everything you can to ensure your executors have all they need to execute your wishes. They can then focus on celebrating your life knowing they won't miss anything and that your wishes will be met.
